About Bored Walk Creations

Bored Walk Creations LLC is a New Jersey-based company that builds and operates high-performance GPU servers for AI, rendering, and technical workloads. We currently run dual RTX 5080, dual RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q, and dual RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition configurations.

Our approach is simple: build it like we would use it ourselves. That means quality hardware, proper cooling and power delivery, transparent operations, and direct communication — not black-box hosting.

Why We're Doing This

After years of personal projects involving AI, 3D printing, electronics, and home automation, we decided to turn our infrastructure into something others can use. BWC Pro exists to give makers, researchers, and professionals access to serious dual-GPU compute without the usual headaches.

Current Focus

We operate multiple dual-GPU systems (consumer 5080s and professional Blackwell 6000s) from East Coast locations. Everything is built and maintained by someone who actually runs these machines for real work.
